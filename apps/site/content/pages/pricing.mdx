## Pressure Washing Pricing Made Simple

Pricing depends on the surfaces being cleaned, the condition of the area, and the size of the job. We provide a clear estimate before work begins.

### What’s Included

- Surface-safe pressure or soft-wash methods based on the material
- Careful setup and protection for landscaping and nearby surfaces
- Rinse-down and tidy wrap-up when the job is complete

### What Can Change the Price

- Total square footage and how many surfaces are included
- Heavy organic buildup (algae, mildew) or staining that needs extra treatment
- Access and obstacles (tight gates, steep driveways, multi-level areas)
- Specialty work (roof soft-wash, gutter clearing, commercial properties)
